Welcome

No more waiting around

At Novyx you join the queue from your own phone. Instead of sitting and wondering how long you'll wait, you can see exactly where you are in line — and we'll text you when you're nearly up. Go for a coffee, run an errand, and come back right on time.

There's nothing to download. Just scan the QR code at the shop with your phone's camera and the page opens in your browser.

How it works

1
Scan & sign in — scan the QR code, then type your name and WhatsApp number.
2
Pick your barber & service — choose who you want and what you'd like done.
3
Join the queue — you get a number and a live view of the line.
4
Come back when it's your turn — watch your spot, get a heads-up, and take the chair.
The big idea Keep the queue page open on your phone. It updates by itself as people ahead of you finish — you never need to refresh, and you won't lose your place.

NOVYX · CUSTOMER GUIDE1 · Tell us who you are
Step 1

Tell us who you are

Where you start: After scanning the QR code, this is the first screen you see.
  1. Type your name in the first box.
  2. Type your WhatsApp number. The +60 is already there, so just enter the rest (for example 12 345 6789).
  3. Tap CONTINUE.
Why we ask for your number We text your receipt and a heads-up to this WhatsApp number — so please make sure it's correct. We won't use it for anything else.
Already in the queue? If you've already joined today, opening the page again takes you straight back to your live queue status — you won't have to type your details twice.
NOVYX · CUSTOMER GUIDE2 · Choose your barber
Step 2

Choose your barber

What you do here: Pick which barber you'd like, and see how busy each one is.

Only barbers who are working right now appear here. The little dot and the text tell you how busy they are:

What you seeWhat it means
Free nowNo wait — you'd be next.
X ahead · short waitA couple of people in front of you.
X ahead · long waitBusy — a longer wait.

Tap the barber you want to move on to their services.

It's live This list updates on its own. If it says "No barbers on shift right now," none are working at the moment — keep the page open and they'll appear when they start.
NOVYX · CUSTOMER GUIDE3 · Pick your service
Step 3

Pick your service & add a code

What you do here: Choose what you'd like done, add a discount code if you have one, and join.
  1. Tap the service you want. The price shows on the right.
  2. Not sure what you want? Tap Custom Service — your barber will decide and set the price (see Chapter 5).
  3. Got a promo code? Type it in DISCOUNT CODE (OPTIONAL). If it's valid you'll see "X% OFF APPLIED" in green and the Total updates.
  4. Tap JOIN QUEUE →.
If a code doesn't work You'll see a short message like "Code not found", "Code expired" or "Limit reached". Double-check the spelling, or just continue without one — a code is always optional.

Step 4

Watch your place in line

The main screen: Once you've joined, this page shows your number and updates live until it's your turn.
On the screenWhat it tells you

2 people ahead of youHow many are still in front of you. This counts down as they finish.

Keep this page open Don't close the tab while you wait — it's how you keep your spot and see live updates. We'll also text your WhatsApp when you're nearly up, so keep your phone handy.
NOVYX · CUSTOMER GUIDE5 · Prices & receipts
Good to know

Prices, discounts & receipts

What this covers: How your price is worked out, and what you'll receive.

5.1 How your total is built

Your booking on the status page always shows a clear breakdown:

LineMeaning
Service priceThe price of the service you picked.
DiscountMoney off from a valid promo code (shown in green).
Add-onAn extra your barber added (e.g. a little beard work).
ReductionAn amount your barber took off.
TotalThe final amount you pay.

5.2 Custom Service

If you chose Custom Service, the price shows as "Set by barber" until your barber finishes and decides it. If you added a discount code, it still applies to whatever price your barber sets.

5.3 Your receipt

We send your receipt to your WhatsApp number — the one you typed at the start. There's nothing you need to do; just check your messages.

Pay at the shop You pay your barber in person as usual. The app just shows the price and keeps your place in line — it doesn't take payment.
NOVYX · CUSTOMER GUIDE6 · Cancel or book again
Step 6

Cancel or book again

What you do here: Leave the queue if your plans change, or start a fresh booking after your cut.

6.1 Changed your mind?

  1. On your status page, tap CANCEL MY SPOT.
  2. Confirm "Cancel your spot in the queue?"
  3. You're removed from the line. You can re-join any time.

You can cancel while you're still waiting. Once you're in the chair, just speak to your barber.

6.2 After your cut

When your service is finished, the screen shows "Service complete. Thanks for visiting!" and a BOOK ANOTHER CUT button — tap it any time you'd like to join the queue again.

Quick tip Wherever you are in the app, the gold button at the bottom takes you where you need to go next — SEE YOUR QUEUE while you're waiting, or BOOK A BARBER to start again.
NOVYX · CUSTOMER GUIDE7 · Take the shop home
Step 7

Take the shop home

What you do here: Browse the grooming products we sell at the shop.

Tap SEE OUR PRODUCTS (the button at the bottom of the screen) any time to see what's on the shelf — pomade, shampoo, serums and more, with prices.

How to buy This page is just to browse. To buy something, simply ask your barber while you're in the chair.
NOVYX · CUSTOMER GUIDEAppendix A · Good to know
Appendix A

Good to know — quick answers

QuestionAnswer
Do I need to install an app?No. Scan the QR code and it opens in your phone's browser.
Will I lose my place if I lock my phone?No — your spot is saved. Just keep the page open so you can see live updates and our text.
How will I know when I'm up?Your number counts down on the screen, and we text your WhatsApp when you're nearly up.
Can I leave and come back?Yes — that's the whole idea. Watch your number and return when it's nearly your turn.
Can I join two barbers at once?No — you hold one spot at a time. Cancel your current spot first if you want to switch.
What if my barber takes a break?You keep your place. New customers just can't join them until they're back.
Where do I pay?In person with your barber, as usual. The app only shows the price.
I used a different phone / cleared my browser.Just scan again and re-enter your name and number to see your queue.

Three things to remember

  • Keep the queue page open while you wait.
  • Check your WhatsApp — that's where your heads-up and receipt arrive.
  • Be ready when it says "It's your turn" — head to the chair.
